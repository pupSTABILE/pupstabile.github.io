The Relocation Path

The geographic trajectory of Jeremy David Stabile, mapping physical movement, traumatic system crashes, and eventual sovereign recovery in the Baltics.

ORIGINS
New Hampshire, USA
Born and raised amidst New England winter storms. Competitive gymnast until a leg break shattered his athletic path. Always dreamed of singing, piano, and creating the exact music he makes today.
TRANSITION
Orlando, FL & Las Vegas, NV
A brief stint under Florida palms followed by months navigating Las Vegas, coping with synthetic lights and early isolation.
THE DESCENT
Los Angeles & Palm Springs, CA
15 years building a professional career in digital architecture while managing complex relational static and quiet anxieties.
MAY 2026 // SYSTEM RESTORE
Tallinn, Estonia
Permanent expat relocation to the Baltics. Finding in Tallinn a clean, tech-forward, and safe sanctuary where his creative reboot could finally bloom in peace.
