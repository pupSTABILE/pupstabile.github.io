The Relocation Path

The geographic trajectory of Jeremy David Stabile, mapping a lifetime of physical movement, traumatic system crashes, and eventual sovereign recovery in the Baltics.

ORIGINS
New Hampshire, USA
Born and raised amidst New England winter storms. Trained as a competitive gymnast until a major leg break during a college scouting high school meet shattered his athletic path, ending his chances of pursuing his biggest dream. For years, he struggled to believe he could be a music artist, but he always dreamed of singing, playing the piano, and now—creating the exact style of music he makes using the advanced digital tools that are finally available today, fusing his technical skillset with raw artistic expression.
TRANSITION
Orlando, FL & Las Vegas, NV
A brief stint under Florida palms followed by a sleepless handful of months navigating the neon deserts of Las Vegas, chasing synthetic lights and coping with the early weight of isolation.
THE DESCENT
Los Angeles & Palm Springs, CA
15 years on the West Coast grid. Building a professional career in digital architecture while managing a complicated LA poly web, relational static, and chronic, quiet anxieties.
SYSTEM CRASH
San Diego, CA
4 years leading to a final breakdown. A devastating 6-week eviction on the physical pavement of San Diego. Surviving at the absolute edge of the system with only his partner and a baseline of human resilience intact.
MAY 2026 // SYSTEM RESTORE
Tallinn, Estonia
Permanent expat relocation to the Baltics. Leaving behind the exhausting social environment of the United States to seek sovereign recovery. Intrigued by Estonia's tech-heavy startup culture and the legal/technical infrastructure of the AI-startup ecosystem, Jeremy found in Tallinn a refreshing, clean, and safe sanctuary—safer than anywhere he has ever lived—where his physical and creative reboot could finally bloom in peace. Here, he authored The System Tetrad: a grand 4-album collection mapping human trauma, recovery, and sovereignty through Architectural Cyber-Trance.

The Sound Protocol

Architectural Cyber-Trance: a sonic philosophy built on heavy underground club architecture and unapologetic human stories.

PROTOCOL_01

The Low-End Throb

Heavy, driving, club-ready Berlin techno and progressive house kick drums that lock in a relentless 126–128 BPM momentum, demanding physical release.

PROTOCOL_02

The Shimmering Grid

Soaring, cascading 90s Euro-trance synthesizer pluck arpeggios and weeping, sidechained analog string pads that create a deep, euphoric melancholy.

PROTOCOL_03

The Biometric Signature

Warm, intimate, close-mic E2 to C#4 bass-baritone vocals, delivered raw and dry without generic tenor pitching to prove authentic human directorship.
