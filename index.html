The Relocation Path

The geographic trajectory of Jeremy David Stabile, mapping a lifetime of physical movement, traumatic system crashes, and eventual sovereign recovery in the Baltics.

ORIGINS
New Hampshire, USA
Born and raised amidst New England winter storms. Trained as a competitive gymnast until a severe high school injury shattered his athletic dreams, driving his kinetic focus into the technical world of cybersecurity.
TRANSITION
Orlando, FL & Las Vegas, NV
A brief stint under Florida palms followed by a sleepless handful of months navigating the neon deserts of Las Vegas, chasing synthetic lights and coping with the early weight of isolation.
THE DESCENT
Los Angeles & Palm Springs, CA
15 years on the West Coast grid. Building a professional career in digital architecture while managing a complicated LA poly web, relational static, and chronic, quiet anxieties.
SYSTEM CRASH
San Diego, CA
4 years leading to a final breakdown. A devastating 6-week eviction on the physical pavement of San Diego. Surviving at the absolute edge of the system with only his partner and a baseline of human resilience intact.
MAY 2026 // SYSTEM RESTORE
Tallinn, Estonia
Permanent expat relocation to the Baltics. Purging US consumerist malware, resetting his legal and financial architecture, and establishing a Technical Office under the 0% corporate tax shield of Harju County.
