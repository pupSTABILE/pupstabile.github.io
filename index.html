The Relocation Path

The physical trajectory of Jeremy David Stabile, mapping movement, system crashes, and eventual sovereign recovery in Estonia.

ORIGINS
New Hampshire, USA
Born and raised amidst New England winter storms. Competitive gymnast until a devastating leg break shattered his athletic path. Fusing technical skillset with raw artistic expression to build his sound.
TRANSITION
Orlando, FL & Las Vegas, NV
A brief stint under Florida palms followed by a sleepless handful of months navigating the neon deserts of Las Vegas, chasing synthetic lights and coping with early isolation.
THE DESCENT
Los Angeles & Palm Springs, CA
15 years on the West Coast grid. Building a professional career in digital architecture while managing complex relational webs, social static, and chronic anxieties.
SYSTEM CRASH
San Diego, CA
A devastating 6-week eviction on the pavement of San Diego. Surviving at the absolute edge of the system with only his partner and baseline human resilience intact.
MAY 2026 // SYSTEM RESTORE
Tallinn, Estonia
Permanent expat relocation to the Baltics. Leaving behind the exhausting social environment of the US for a clean, safe, and tech-forward sanctuary where his physical and creative reboot could bloom in peace.

The Sound Protocol

Architectural Cyber-Trance: a sonic philosophy built on heavy underground club architecture and raw human stories.

PROTOCOL_01

The Low-End Throb

Heavy, driving, club-ready Berlin progressive house & techno kick drums that lock in a relentless 126–128 BPM momentum, demanding physical release.

PROTOCOL_02

The Shimmering Grid

Soaring, cascading 90s Euro-trance synthesizer pluck arpeggios and weeping analog string pads that create a deep, euphoric melancholy.

PROTOCOL_03

The Biometric Signature

Warm, intimate, close-mic E2 to C#4 bass-baritone vocals, delivered raw and dry without pitch-shifting to prove authentic human directorship.
