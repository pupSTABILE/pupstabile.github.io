The Relocation Path

The geographic trajectory of Jeremy David Stabile, mapping physical movement, traumatic system crashes, and sovereign recovery in Estonia.

ORIGINS
New Hampshire, USA
Born amidst New England storms. Trained as a competitive gymnast until a major leg break during a college scouting meet shattered his athletic path. Fused technical computer skillsets with piano and vocal passion to begin composing.
TRANSITION
Orlando, FL & Las Vegas, NV
Brief stint under Florida palms followed by months navigating Las Vegas, chasing synthetic lights while developing digital architecture skills.
THE DESCENT
Los Angeles & Palm Springs, CA
15 years building a digital architecture career while managing West Coast relational static and chronic anxieties.
SYSTEM CRASH
San Diego, CA
Final breakdown leading to a devastating 6-week eviction on the physical pavement of San Diego. Surviving at the system's edge with resilience intact.
MAY 2026 // SYSTEM RESTORE
Tallinn, Estonia
Permanent expat relocation to the Baltics. Intrigued by Estonia's tech startup infrastructure, Jeremy found in Tallinn a clean, safe sanctuary where his creative reboot could bloom in peace.

The Sound Protocol

Architectural Cyber-Trance: a sonic philosophy built on heavy underground club architecture and unapologetic human stories.

PROTOCOL_01

The Low-End Throb

Heavy, driving, club-ready Berlin techno and progressive house kick drums locking in 126–128 BPM momentum.

PROTOCOL_02

The Shimmering Grid

Soaring, cascading 90s Euro-trance plucks and weeping, sidechained analog string pads creating deep euphoric melancholy.

PROTOCOL_03

The Biometric Signature

Warm, intimate, close-mic E2 to C#4 bass-baritone vocals delivered raw and dry without generic tenor pitching.
