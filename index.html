The Relocation Path

The geographic trajectory of Jeremy David Stabile, mapping a lifetime of physical movement, traumatic system crashes, and eventual sovereign recovery in the Baltics.

ORIGINS
New Hampshire, USA
Born and raised amidst New England winter storms. Trained as a competitive gymnast until a major leg break during a college scouting meet shattered his athletic path. Always dreamed of singing, playing piano, and creating the exact sound he makes today using modern digital tools.
TRANSITION
Orlando, FL & Las Vegas, NV
A brief stint under Florida palms followed by a sleepless handful of months navigating the neon deserts of Las Vegas, chasing synthetic lights and coping with early isolation.
THE DESCENT
Los Angeles & Palm Springs, CA
15 years on the West Coast grid. Building a career in digital architecture while managing a complicated LA poly web, relational static, and chronic, quiet anxieties.
SYSTEM CRASH
San Diego, CA
4 years leading to a final breakdown. A devastating 6-week eviction on the pavement of San Diego. Surviving at the absolute edge of the system with only his partner and human resilience intact.
MAY 2026 // SYSTEM RESTORE
Tallinn, Estonia
Permanent expat relocation to the Baltics. Intrigued by Estonia's tech startup culture and legal AI infrastructure, Jeremy found in Tallinn a refreshing, clean, and safe sanctuary where his creative reboot could bloom in peace.

The Sound Protocol

Architectural Cyber-Trance: a sonic philosophy built on heavy club architecture and authentic human stories.

PROTOCOL_01

The Low-End Throb

Heavy, driving, club-ready Berlin techno and progressive house kick drums that lock in a relentless 126–128 BPM momentum.

PROTOCOL_02

The Shimmering Grid

Soaring 90s Euro-trance pluck arpeggios and weeping, sidechained analog synth pads that create euphoric melancholy.

PROTOCOL_03

The Biometric Signature

Warm, intimate, close-mic E2 to C#4 bass-baritone lead vocals, delivered raw and dry without generic tenor pitching.
